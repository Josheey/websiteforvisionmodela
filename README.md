# Visionary Models

Create a clean, modern one-page website for Home Vision Models, an Australian business based in Mount Gambier, South Australia. We create 3D-printed architectural scale models of houses for residential builders and developers across Australia.

Use a premium minimalist architectural style with white, charcoal and grey colours.

Include these sections:

Hero section with “Bring Your Home Designs to Life”
About Home Vision Models
Builder Solutions
How It Works
Models & Packages
Furniture & Accessories
Gallery with placeholder images
FAQ
Contact / Request a Quote

Explain that our models can have removable roofs and miniature furniture, vehicles and household items so customers can understand the floor plan and experiment with furniture placement before construction.

Add the email sales@homevisionmodels.com and make the website responsive for desktop and mobile. Use professional architectural imagery/placeholders and avoid bright colours.
